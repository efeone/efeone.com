Privacy Policy

Last updated: 02/02/2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how EFEONE PRIVATE LIMITED (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when users interact with our platform. Our platform integrates with third-party services including Meta (WhatsApp Business API) and LinkedIn Lead Sync API.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following types of information:

  • Contact details such as name, phone number, email address, company name, and job title.
  • Lead information submitted through LinkedIn Lead Forms.
  • Message content exchanged via WhatsApp Business API.
  • Metadata such as message timestamps, delivery status, and interaction logs.
  • System logs and audit data related to API integrations.

3. WhatsApp Business API (Meta) Data Usage

When using WhatsApp Business API integration, we process messages and related data strictly to enable business communication between organizations and their customers.

  • Messages are sent and received only based on user-initiated interactions or approved message templates.
  • Message data is not used for advertising or marketing without explicit user consent.
  • We do not store WhatsApp message content longer than necessary to provide the service.
  • All WhatsApp data is handled in compliance with Meta’s Platform Policies.

4. How We Use the Information

The collected information is used only for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing leads in ERP/CRM systems such as ERPNext.
  • Customer communication via WhatsApp Business API.
  • Sales follow-ups, customer support, and service notifications.
  • Internal reporting, analytics, and operational improvements.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. Data may be shared only:

  • With authorized users within the organization.
  • With service providers strictly necessary to operate the platform.
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process.

6. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Access to sensitive data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

7. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as required to fulfill business, contractual, or legal obligations. Data may be anonymized or deleted upon request, subject to applicable laws.

8. User Rights

Users have the right to:

  • Access their personal data.
  • Request correction or deletion of their data.
  • Withdraw consent for communications.
  • Request restriction of data processing where applicable.

9. Third-Party Platforms

Our platform integrates with third-party platforms such as Meta (WhatsApp Business API) and LinkedIn. Data obtained through these platforms is processed in accordance with their respective terms, policies, and applicable data protection laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
